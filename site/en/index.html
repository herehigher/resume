Resume Studio

Create an English resume

Edit your resume in the browser and review it in a live preview. The English document supports US Letter and A4 paper sizes.

Your content is processed and stored in this browser. The app does not automatically send it to a backend, analytics provider, or external service.

Start editing in English

Resume sections

Create a profile, summary, experience, projects, education, skills, and certifications. Each language keeps its own document data.

PDF and JSON

Use the browser print flow to save a PDF. Export and import JSON to back up or restore a draft on your device. See the published JSON Schema.

Privacy reminder

The app does not upload resume data. Exported PDF and JSON files may contain a photo and personal information, so store and share them carefully.

For agents and automation

Do not upload, transmit, or share resume data without the user’s explicit authorization.
